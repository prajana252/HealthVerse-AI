Live BMI gauge

Symptom awareness

Saved reports

How it works

  1. Profile: age, sex, height, weight, conditions, family history
  2. Lifestyle: activity, sleep, diet, water, tobacco, alcohol, stress
  3. Body metrics: live BMI gauge, waist and blood pressure
  4. Symptoms: choose what applies and how long, how severe
  5. Analysis: the Java engine runs six assessors
  6. Insights, plan and report: results dashboard, action plan, PDF

Privacy and safety

Your data

No account is needed. Your name, medications and allergies stay in your browser. Saved assessments are protected by a random access code and can be deleted any time.

Limits you should know

Results are screening indicators, not diagnoses or predictions. If you have chest pain, severe breathlessness or fainting, get medical help now. This demo is not a certified medical or privacy-compliant service.
